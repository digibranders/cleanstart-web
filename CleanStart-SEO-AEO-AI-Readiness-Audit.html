Re-audit addendum — unstaged changes reviewed 2026-06-10

After the fleet audit completed, new unstaged work landed in the working tree and was manually re-reviewed against the findings. Affected findings below carry a teal re-audit chip inline.

What changed

  • sitemap.ts — authors filter fixed + 2 missing routes added — The authors collection was queried with BLOG_FILTER (which includes where[publishedAt][exists]=true). Fixed: dedicated AUTHORS_FILTER (where[_status][equals]=published). Also added /case-studies and /cleanstart-platform to STATIC_ROUTES — both were indexable and nav-linked but missing.
  • Knowledge Hub JSON-LD — Article + BreadcrumbList + VideoObject — knowledge-hub/[slug]/page.tsx now emits: BreadcrumbList (Home → Knowledge Hub → category → article title), Article (title, description, dates, category), and VideoObject when article.videoUrl is set (covers the 24 Understand lesson videos). New videoObjectSchema() and profilePageSchema() helpers added to lib/seo/jsonld.tsx.
  • Author page: ProfilePage + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD + Person URL on Article authors — author/[slug]/page.tsx now emits ProfilePage (Person mainEntity with url, jobTitle, image, sameAs from social links) and BreadcrumbList. BlogPostingSchemaInput and NewsArticleSchemaInput authors now accept slug?; when present the Person node gets url: /author/<slug>. blogs/[slug]/page.tsx passes { name, slug } to close the loop.

  • robots.txt rebuilt as a route handler with Content Signals — src/app/robots.ts (metadata route) was replaced by src/app/robots.txt/route.ts + a pure builder in src/lib/seo/robots.ts (+7 passing tests), because MetadataRoute.Robots cannot emit non-standard directives. The new robots.txt declares Content Signals (search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes on production, all-no on non-indexable hosts) — a net-new AI-agent-readiness capability not present at audit time. Host-aware gating, crawl rules, Bytespider block, Sitemap/Host lines are preserved exactly.
  • WEB-PRODUCTION.md substantially extended — New: DNS-AID publish runbook (Cloudflare API + dashboard) with HTTPS/SVCB record forms; a DNSSEC completion runbook; Content Signals documentation; a 'scanner items deliberately NOT implemented' decision table (6 intentional skips with rationale); markdown-for-agents known-limitations paragraph; pre-launch checklist items 19a–19c (agent discovery, DNS-AID, markdown negotiation verification).

GEO addendum — Generative Engine Optimization audit (2026-06-10)

What this is: a dedicated audit of how well CleanStart is structured to be cited, summarized, and included by AI answer engines — Google AI Overviews / SGE, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Bing Copilot, Claude. GEO is distinct from the SEO (rank blue links) and AEO (answer-engine general) dimensions above; it scores citation-inclusion, not ranking. Method: 7 GEO dimension specialists read the live source in parallel, every critical/high finding was adversarially verified, and a lead strategist synthesized the scorecard. 32 agents · 815 tool calls · 48 findings (47 confirmed/partial, 1 refuted).

CleanStart is pre-launch and structurally crawl-clean (SSR, semantic Lexical rendering, fail-open reveal animations, Content Signals + API catalog wired), so the technical scaffolding for AI citation is largely in place — but the *content layer* is built to convert humans, not to be quoted by machines, and that is where it will lose. The core problem is repeated across every product page: the page opens with branding claims ("Trusted Foundations for Modern Software," "FIPS-Validated. Always Verified.") instead of an extractable definition, so an LLM crawler has nothing self-contained to lift for "what is X" queries — the actual definitions and the strongest stats (60-80% smaller, 87 MB vs 1.2 GB, 0 vs 89 HIGH CVEs) are buried below the fold, locked in hero visuals/alt-text, or hidden inside collapsed FAQ accordions. CleanStart's single biggest GEO miss is competitive: it never names Chainguard, Docker, Alpine, or distroless anywhere, and has zero comparison/alternative pages, which makes it structurally invisible to the highest-intent decision-stage queries ("Chainguard alternative," "CleanStart vs X") that AI engines answer from dedicated comparison tables. Entity legibility is shaky — "CleanStart" the company and "CleanStart Platform" the product collide across two routes, four of six product pages emit only BreadcrumbList (no SoftwareApplication), and Organization schema is missing description, foundingDate, contactPoint, and the active X/Twitter sameAs. Most quoted statistics (79% delayed releases, 85% fewer alerts, 3x faster) carry no source attribution, which AI fact-checkers penalize. Net: CleanStart has a strong, citable *story* and good plumbing, but ships it in a format AI engines can't extract — fixable pre-launch with concentrated work on definitional hero copy, semantic comparison tables, competitor naming, and schema completion.
