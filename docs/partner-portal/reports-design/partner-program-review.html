Method and definitions

How the figures were produced, so any number in this report can be checked in the portal.

Registered deal
A deal a partner submitted in the period, counted on the day it was submitted.
Approved
A registered deal accepted by CleanStart. Won deals are also approved.
Won
A deal marked closed won in HubSpot.
Win rate
Won deals divided by won plus lost deals. Open deals are left out, so the rate moves as they close.
Registered value
The sum of USD values on registered deals. Deals with no USD value, or a value in another currency, are counted as deals but add nothing to value.
Time to decision
Calendar days from registration to the first accept or reject. After a rejection and resubmission the clock restarts at the resubmission. The portal Overview shows an average in business days, so its figure differs from the median here.
Protected now
Deals currently inside their protection window.
Comparison
The prior period has the same length and ends the day before this one starts: 28 May to 25 Aug 2026, 90 days.
