# Partner portal completeness audit

Scope: `apps/portal` (read only, nothing run). Evidence paths are relative to `apps/portal/src` and line numbers were re-checked with grep. Items already fixed in `docs/partner-portal/UX-AUDIT.md` (text Accept and Reject buttons, partner detail scroll, `?denied=1` toast, empty states, mobile tabs) are not repeated. Phase 2 omissions (tiers, NFR, training, MDF, referral payouts: master plan line 23) and legal-blocked items (agreement text and PDF: OVERNIGHT-REPORT line 173, PLAN-REVIEW line 49) are marked "known". The proposal itself is not in the repo (the master plan links an external artifact, line 15), so "promised" below means master plan lines 21 and 23 plus the copy the screens print.

Two corrections to the brief: there is no `/admin/account` route (see AACC-01), and registrations never store deal value, country or industry (see REG-01), so those are "not stored, not collected" rather than "stored but hidden".

## Summary of the biggest gaps

1. Staff have no Account page at all: no route, no menu item. Staff cannot change password, enable two-step, see sessions or edit their profile in-app (AACC-01).
2. Deal registration collects only customer, website, one contact (no role), products, region, manager, free-text close date and notes. No deal value, country, industry, use case, competitor or seats exist in the schema (REG-01). No reference number, receipt, confirmation email or PDF after submit (REG-05).
3. Partners cannot edit, add a contact to, or withdraw a registration. No action exists (DET-02, DET-03).
4. Staff can only see waiting deals. There is no staff deal list, no status facets and no deal detail; search hits and notifications land on the bare queue (APR-01, APR-02, SHELL-05).
5. Partner admins cannot edit a member's name, email, title or phone, and role change is silent, unconfirmed and unavailable for invited people (TEAM-01, TEAM-02). Staff cannot manage a partner's people at all (PTN-04).
6. The partner agreement shown at accept-invite is placeholder text, the PDF button is a toast, and the "you will be asked to accept a new version" promise has no code (INV-01, CO-03, CO-04). Known, legal-blocked.
7. The video "Watch" button opens a black box that plays nothing; library rows have no thumbnails, no grouping, and summary and duration can never be entered (DOC-01, DOC-02, ADOC-02).
8. Several server actions have no UI (staff region change, staff invite resend) and many accept staff-on-behalf `partnerId` inputs that no screen uses (section "Server actions with no UI").
9. Applicants cannot check status after leaving the done page (1 hour cookie) and the form lacks partner type detail, current security vendors and products of interest (DONE-01, APPLY-01). No digest email exists for reviewers (APR-08).
10. Reports are all-time counts only, titled "Quarter summary" and "Pipeline", with no value, range, review-time or funnel metrics (REP-01 to REP-04).

Severity: P1 blocks a real user, P2 clearly incomplete, P3 polish. Effort: S under half a day, M 1 to 2 days, L more.
