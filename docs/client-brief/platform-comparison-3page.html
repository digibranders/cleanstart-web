Platform Modernization · Client Brief

From Webflow to a Custom Platform

We replaced a template-bound Webflow site with a purpose-built Next.js application and our own content platform — keeping full control of speed, search visibility, data, and cost.

What changed

Every row below is a pain point we removed on the left and the capability we now own on the right. Same content, same brand — a faster, more flexible, and fully owned foundation.

Area Before — Webflow Now — Custom Next.js + CMS
Performance Builder-generated markup with limited control over how pages render and load. Next.js server & static rendering with optimized images — fast, consistent Core Web Vitals.
SEO & discoverability Template-level SEO controls; little say over structured data and indexing. Per-page metadata, auto sitemaps, JSON-LD, instant IndexNow pings and AI-bot rules.
Content authoring Fixed field types and plan-capped CMS items; rigid editor workflow. Tailored content models with drafts, live preview, a publish checklist and scheduling.
Design & brand Constrained to the Webflow builder; designs bent to fit the tool. Figma-to-code on a shared design system — pixel-exact, no template ceiling.
Integrations & automation Limited backend logic; integrations stitched on as third-party bolt-ons. Built-in HubSpot, MS Teams, webhooks & analytics, managed from an editor dashboard.
Lead reliability Form submissions can be lost during a provider outage. Resilient lead pipeline with an automatic fallback queue — no lead lost.
Search Basic keyword search with no insight into what visitors look for. Full-text search (Meilisearch) with query analytics.
Security & compliance Platform-managed security with little room to harden or extend. CSP + Cloudflare WAF, rate limiting, bot protection and GDPR tooling.
Cost & ownership Per-seat and per-item fees, with content locked into the platform. Own the code and the data, self-hosted, with no per-item limits.

Automation across the platform

What runs on autopilot

The work the platform does for you — across the CMS and the website — so the team ships content, not chores.

Automated end-to-end

Built in, not bolted on — no third-party plugins, embeds, or add-on subscriptions anywhere.

On every publish

  • Table of contents, reading time & word count computed
  • Every change saved as a version — full history & one-click rollback
  • Old URLs auto-redirect when a slug changes
  • The team is notified in Microsoft Teams

Leads & forms

  • Every submission captured with a fallback queue — no lead lost
  • Relayed to HubSpot CRM automatically
  • Confirmation & internal emails sent (Brevo)
  • Careers applications routed to HR with the résumé attached
  • Spam & bots blocked at submit (Turnstile)

Images & media

  • Uploads auto-converted to WebP; served as AVIF/WebP
  • Thumb / card / hero sizes generated on upload
  • Responsive, lazy-loaded & sized per device (next/image)
  • Focal-point smart-crop for thumbnails & social cards
  • Stored & delivered from the Cloudflare R2 CDN

Authoring & formatting

  • Rich editor: headings, lists, tables, callouts, code & embeds
  • Pasted content auto-cleaned into consistent formatting
  • Lists & spacing normalised on save
  • Inline media with SEO-friendly, human-readable filenames

Search

  • New & edited content indexed instantly (Meilisearch)
  • Nightly drift check self-heals the index
  • Search queries logged for content analytics

SEO & discoverability

  • Per-page titles, descriptions & canonical URLs
  • Rich-result schema — articles, FAQs, breadcrumbs, jobs (JSON-LD)
  • Open Graph / X cards + auto-generated share images
  • Sitemaps, robots & AI-bot rules generated
  • Re-rendered on change (ISR) · instant IndexNow pings

Privacy & compliance

  • Personal data redacted on a retention schedule
  • Résumés deleted after the retention window
  • GDPR erasure honoured across connected systems
  • Cookie consent enforced (GA4 Consent Mode v2)

Reliability & monitoring

  • Failed webhooks retried automatically
  • Broken links scanned every night
  • Errors tracked with personal data scrubbed (Sentry)
  • Nightly database backups
